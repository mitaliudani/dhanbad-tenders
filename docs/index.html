Kaise use karein
  1. Upar ke dabbe dabaiye: Open for bidding matlab abhi bid lag sakti hai, Closing in 3 days matlab jaldi band honge.
  2. Search box mein item ka naam likhiye, jaise cable, MCCB, wire, bearing.
  3. Filters dabakar buyer (BCCL, ECL Mugma, Railways…) aur area chuniye.
  4. Details & specs dabane par saare items, quantity, specification, EMD aur delivery ke din dikhte hain.
  5. Bid PDF (GeM) dabane par GeM ki asli bid PDF khulti hai. Bid lagane se pehle wahi padhiye.
  6. ☆ dabakar tender save kijiye. Saved tender ★ Saved mein milenge.
  7. Ye list roz do baar (lagbhag subah 8 aur shaam 6 baje) apne aap update hoti hai. Upar Updated ka time dekhiye.

Where the data comes from. Public tender listings (Tender247, TenderShark, Tenderlelo) read by Claude twice a day, around 7:50 AM and 5:50 PM, plus any GeM bid alert emails, checked every 3 hours. Only Dhanbad district bids are shown. "Bid PDF (GeM)" opens the official bid document on bidplus.gem.gov.in; "Search on GeM" opens GeM's bid search, where you paste the bid number.

Always check the bid PDF and any corrigendum on GeM before you bid. Closing dates and quantities can change after the last refresh. Bids older than two months are removed automatically.
